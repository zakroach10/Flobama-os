FloBama Mac Camera 1.4.3

IMPORTANT — replace any older install
1. Quit Cam ● / any old FloBama Mac Camera process
2. Eject every disk named FloBama Mac Camera
3. Delete /Applications/FloBama Mac Camera.app
4. Download Mac Camera 1.4.3 from FloBama OS → Cameras
5. Open THIS disk (title must say FloBama Mac Camera 1.4.3)
6. Drag into Applications, then open it

Cam ● only says Connected after FloBama OS accepts a heartbeat.
If Cameras says “no heartbeat”, the Mac menu bar should also show offline.

Local Network: allow “FloBama Mac Camera” when prompted.
You cannot manually add apps to that privacy list — open this app once so it appears.

Logs: ~/Library/Logs/FloBamaMacConnector/connector.log
