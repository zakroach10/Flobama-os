FloBama Mac Camera 1.4.2

IMPORTANT — replace any older install
1. Quit any old FloBama Mac Camera / Terminal windows and Cam ● menu item
2. Eject every disk named FloBama Mac Camera
3. Delete /Applications/FloBama Mac Camera.app
4. Download Mac Camera 1.4.2 from FloBama OS → Cameras
5. Open THIS disk (title must say FloBama Mac Camera 1.4.2)
6. Drag into Applications, then open it

This version runs as the FloBama Mac Camera app (not Terminal).
When macOS asks for Local Network, click Allow for “FloBama Mac Camera”.
You cannot manually add apps to that privacy list — opening this app creates the entry.

Look for Cam ● in the menu bar. Click it to see discovered NDI source names.
The same list appears in FloBama OS → Cameras under “NDI sources on Mac”.

Logs: ~/Library/Logs/FloBamaMacConnector/connector.log
